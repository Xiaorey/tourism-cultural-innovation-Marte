A living tradition

The Moriones Festival

Each Holy Week, Marinduque's towns come alive with the Moriones: costumed performers wearing hand-crafted Roman-inspired masks and armor. Rooted in the story of Longinus, the tradition brings faith, artistry, and community together in a celebration passed from one generation to the next.

Tourism, with imagination

Keep the culture close.
Let new ideas grow.

Cultural innovation starts by listening to the people who carry a place's stories. Here are a few ways tourism can help those stories thrive.

Digital island guide

A lightweight, mobile-friendly guide can pair local stories, walking routes, accessibility notes, and practical visitor tips.

Local businesses online

Help makers, homestays, guides, and food businesses share their offers through a community-curated directory.

Culture, shared fairly

Co-create experiences with residents so their knowledge is credited, valued, and benefits stay local.
